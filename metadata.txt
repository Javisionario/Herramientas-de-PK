about=PK Tools provides QGIS tools to identify, locate and measure positions along M-enabled line layers. It is designed for road workflows based on kilometre posts (PK), but it also supports generic M-enabled layers and raw M values. The plugin includes tools to click and identify positions, locate a specific PK or M value, export temporary points and measure distances conservatively along the selected geometry. It does not create or fix M values; it works with existing M-enabled geometries.
